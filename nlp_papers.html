Staff-Recommended Papers for NLP/LLM Course

This is a curated list of papers recommended by the course staff. Feel free to explore!

2024

  • TOFU: A Task of Fictitious Unlearning for LLMs - Maini et al.
  • Self-Explore: Enhancing Mathematical Reasoning in Language Models with Fine-grained Rewards - Hwang et al.
  • Refusal in Language Models Is Mediated by a Single Direction - Arditi et al.
  • From Theft to Bomb-Making: The Ripple Effect of Unlearning in Defending Against Jailbreak Attacks - Zhang et al.
  • Physics of Language Models: Part 2.1, Grade-School Math and the Hidden Reasoning Process - Ye et al.

2023

  • On the Blind Spots of Model-Based Evaluation Metrics for Text Generation - He et al.
  • Can Language Models Solve Graph Problems in Natural Language? - Wang et al.
  • EconAgent: Large Language Model-Empowered Agents for Simulating Macroeconomic Activities - Liao et al.
  • Detecting Pretraining Data from Large Language Models - Shi et al.
  • The Earth is Flat because...: Investigating LLMs' Belief towards Misinformation via Persuasive Conversation - Xu et al.
